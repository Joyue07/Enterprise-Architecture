### Enterprise Architecture & Business Modeling

**Academic Project | Enterprise Computing and ERP Systems**  
Stockholm University | November 2026

---

## 1. Project Overview

Skyrealms: Shattered Isles is a conceptual enterprise architecture project focused on designing a fictional MMORPG company and its supporting business ecosystem.

The project explores how business strategy, value networks, organizational goals, business processes, and IT systems can be integrated into a coherent enterprise architecture.

The proposed company adopts a differentiation-focused strategy, offering AI-enhanced cooperative gameplay with a fair, non-pay-to-win monetization model.

## 2. Project Scope

The project covers the following areas:

| Area | Methods & Deliverables |
|---|---|
| Value Network Analysis | VDML |
| Goal Design | Business Motivation Model (BMM), Porter's Five Forces |
| Process Design | REA Ontology, UML, Event-driven Process Chains (EPC) |
| IT Architecture | ERP, CRM, BPMS, EAI, Data Warehouse, BI |
| Business Performance Management | Leading and Lagging KPIs |
| Enterprise Architecture | ArchiMate |
| IT Risk Assessment | NVD vulnerability analysis and business impact assessment |

## 3. Key Deliverables

### 3.1 Value Network Analysis

Designed a value network covering players, digital distribution platforms, third-party creators, cloud providers, AI vendors, ISPs, and the Game Forum.

### 3.2 Business Goals & Strategy

Developed a BMM goal model aligned with Porter's Five Forces and a differentiation-focused business strategy.

### 3.3 Business Process Design

Modelled key economic exchanges using REA and designed operational workflows using EPC, including:

- Game sales and subscriptions
- 3D creator asset procurement
- Third-party partner licensing
- LLM service procurement
- Steam release management
- Cloud and ISP service procurement

### 3.4 IT Architecture

Proposed a modular IT architecture integrating operational systems, ERP, CRM, BPMS, EAI, data warehousing, BI, and dashboards.

### 3.5 Business Performance Management

Defined 10 leading and lagging KPIs, linking business objectives, operational activities, source systems, and dashboard data flows.

### 3.6 Enterprise Architecture & Security

Created an ArchiMate enterprise architecture model and assessed how reported software vulnerabilities could propagate through application systems and business processes to affect organizational goals.

Proposed mitigation measures to address critical security risks and improve system resilience.
